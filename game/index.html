Tips
Crimes raise your wanted stars. Break line of sight and stay out of the searchlight to cool things down. The safehouse (green house icon) saves your game, heals you and clears your wanted level. Spend cash at Marv's Motors (gold icon) on faster cars — they wait for you at the safehouse.
